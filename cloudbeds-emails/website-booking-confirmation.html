Good to know before your stay

  • Check-in is from 2:00 PM and check-out is at 11:30 AM. Arriving early? You're welcome to relax at the Pavilion — your first meal is lunch. Early check-in (from 10:00 AM) and late check-out (until 3:00 PM) are subject to availability at ₱750 per hour.
  • Full-board meals are required for every guest each night — lunch on arrival, dinner, and breakfast the next morning (Breakfast 7:00–10:00 AM · Lunch 12:00 NN–2:00 PM · Dinner 7:00–9:00 PM). If they aren't on your booking yet, they're settled at check-in: Adult (13+) ₱2,695 · Child (6–12) ₱1,347.50 · Child (0–5) free, per night.
  • Every guest must be present at check-in with a valid government-issued ID. Senior Citizens / PWDs: please bring your ID for the 20% discount.
  • Tourism Ecological Fee: ₱50.00 per visitor, collected by the Municipality of San Juan at its booth at Km 3, Buhay na Sapa, San Juan (or at our Front Office).
  • Outside food & drinks are not allowed, except chips, biscuits and bottled water. Corkage applies to specific beverages.
  • Pets are welcome (max. two) with their vaccination card and our Pet Policy — ₱750 per pet, per night.
  • Drivers, nannies & bodyguards: ₱1,000 per night for quarters plus ₱750 per night for meals — please let us know in advance.
